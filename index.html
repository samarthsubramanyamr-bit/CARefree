I am Samarth, a student founder of CARefree. A student of Vydehi School of Excellence

Living in Bangalore, a city of traffic, I realised that there is a lack of a proper doorstep service for Vehicle maintenance, specifically cars. My family, owning a car which has very less service centres India, have to go to local garages or make-do with other brand service centres. Car brands like Chevrolet, Ford and some luxury brands have very less service centres in India. Other than this, people who live in Tier 2/3 cities sometimes have to travel 70+km to find a service centre. There is also a lot of time spent in maintaining a car. That is why, I have come up with CARefree, a doorstep car maintenance service so that you don't have to spend hours looking for a service centre and hours again going to the service centre. With CARefree, you can book doorstep car maintenance service and even get repair works get done at your doorstep. Soon, there will also be an online journal of your car's maintenance records. I will be starting with a prototype, which is a WhatsApp group with Mechanics from local garages and people who want to use the service. In the group, people can contact the mechanics and book services at their doorstep. No need to travel long distances anymore or spend hours looking for a service centre for your car.

So that you can live carefree.
