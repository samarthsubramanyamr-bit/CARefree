Living in Bangalore, a city notorious for its traffic, I realized there is a critical gap in proper doorstep service for vehicle maintenance, specifically cars.

My family owns a car brand that has very few service centers left in India. Like many owners of brands like Chevrolet, Ford, or luxury imports, we often have to rely on generic local garages or make-do with other brand service centers. For people living in Tier 2/3 cities, it's even worse—sometimes requiring a 70+ km journey just to find a center.

CARefree changes that. We are launching a doorstep car maintenance and repair service alongside an online digital journal for your car’s service history. We are starting lean with a verified WhatsApp community connecting you directly to skilled local mechanics.
